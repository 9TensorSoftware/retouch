Photo and video editor · Free and open source

A darkroom in your browser.

Crop, adjust, cut out and erase, with AI that runs on your own device, so your photos never leave it. No install, no account, no upload.

Try it

Your photos stay on your device.

Here are five photos to start with. Click one to edit it, or drop in your own. They stay in this tab, and nothing is uploaded.

Adjust · Curves · Color mix

Change one color. Leave the rest alone.

Fourteen adjustments, tone curves for each channel and an eight-band color mixer. Here the maples turn from orange to crimson; the sky and the road don't move.

Looks and filters

Try a look. Then take it apart.

A look is only settings: a filter and a few sliders you can keep adjusting. Every preview is drawn from your own photo. These five come from a plugin a few dozen lines long, built on the same API you'd use.

AI, on your device

AI that never sees a server.

Each model downloads once, then runs on your own graphics card or processor. Your photos stay put, and there's no account or API key to set up. These results came out of Rétouch on these photos.

And the rest

Everything you'd reach for.

Every edit stays adjustable until you're done, with undo all the way back to the original.

  • Crop and straighten

    Aspect presets, a straighten ruler, quarter turns and flips.

  • Perspective and lens

    Straighten leaning buildings and undo lens distortion.

  • Masks

    Darken a sky or warm a face with linear and radial masks.

  • Liquify

    Push, pull and smooth with a brush.

  • Stylize

    Tilt-shift, duotone, halftone, posterize and pixelate.

  • Video

    Trim, crop, adjust and change the speed, or save a GIF. Encoded in the browser.

  • Ask AI

    Press ⌘K and describe the edit you want. Bring your own Anthropic key.

  • Phones and tablets

    A bottom sheet, tools you can swipe through, and pinch to zoom.

  • Batch export

    Edit a whole set, then save every photo in one go.

For developers

Drop it into your own app.

Rétouch is a TypeScript library with no framework to adopt. Mount the gallery and editor in any element, add tools with the same controls the built-in ones use, and restyle everything with CSS custom properties.

$ npm i @9tensor/retouch
  • Read the docs
  • Write a plugin
  • Theme it
import { Retouch } from "@9tensor/retouch";
import { installMlTools } from "@9tensor/retouch-ml";

const retouch = new Retouch({
  target: "#editor",
  theme: "auto",
  onDone: (blobs) => upload(blobs),
});

// Optional: cutout, erase, upscale and the rest, on-device.
installMlTools(retouch);
